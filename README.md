# Synthetic_Sales_Data_Analytics
This dataset is a realistic simulation of online retail transactions and contains 1,000 rows and 13 key attributes. Each row represents a single product purchase line item (not a full invoice), allowing granular analysis of customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends. 

DATA COVERAGE SUMMARY
Temporal Range: Covers multiple months across at least two years.

Customer Base: Diverse, with a wide age distribution (15–70+) and mixed gender representation.

Categories: Five major product categories, representative of typical retail segments.

Geography: Multiple cities are represented, allowing regional trend analysis.

Payment Modes: Includes common online retail payment methods.

BUSINESS USE CASES ENABLED BY THIS DATASET
Sales Forecasting – Time-series revenue forecasting (monthly, category-wise).

Customer Segmentation – Based on age, gender, geography, and spending.

Product Strategy – Analyze product/category performance over time.

Promotional Targeting – Identify underperforming months and customer segments.

Operational Planning – Determine inventory needs based on seasonal spikes.

--Though synthetic, this dataset is rich and well-structured, making it ideal for:

--Practicing real-world data analytics techniques.

--Implementing predictive and prescriptive models.

--Simulating business decisions based on analytical insights.
